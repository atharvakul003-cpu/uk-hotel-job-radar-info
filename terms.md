# Terms of use

UK Hotel Job Radar is a personal monitoring pilot for its owner. It is not open for public sign-up and is not affiliated with the monitored employers or their recruitment platforms.

Job alerts are informational. Availability, posting dates and eligibility may be incomplete or change. The owner should verify details on the official application page. Monitoring and email delivery may be delayed or interrupted; same-day detection is not guaranteed.

The tool does not submit applications or make recruitment decisions. Its owner controls Google authorization and GitHub scheduling and can stop monitoring at any time.

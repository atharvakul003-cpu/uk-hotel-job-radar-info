# UK Hotel Job Radar

UK Hotel Job Radar is a personal-use tool that checks official Recruit Genie vacancies for Aimbridge, Apex and Dakota and emails newly detected relevant jobs to its owner's nominated address. It is not a public recruitment service and does not accept applications.

Google authorization verifies the sender's email identity and permits sending alerts and a labelled connection test. The tool does not request access to read the Gmail inbox.
